Artificial Intelligence Assurance Protocol v6.5

From Task Design to Claim-Level Assurance. AIAP asks what a consequential assessment genuinely allows an institution to claim about a student, then aligns the claim, evidence-producing condition, assurance route, evidence, decision rule, and programme or public use.

Read the Core Paper Read the Standard Download Complete Release GitHub Release Release Guide

Core claim: consequential assessment must specify what the student is claimed to know or do, what evidence supports that claim, and the conditions under which the inference is defensible.

Four assurance routes

Lane 1 · Secured

Personally demonstrated capability under materially constrained conditions.

Lane 2A · Competence-authenticated open

Complex open work paired with claim-targeted authentication of present competence.

Lane 2B · Open non-certifying

Learning or feedback activity that does not silently acquire certification authority.

Lane 3 · AI-integrated open

Responsible AI-mediated judgement, verification, control, and accountability as part of the construct.

What AIAP adds

Claim → condition → assurance route → evidence → combination rule → inference → programme/public use.

The release includes the canonical paper, Normative Assurance Standard, implementation handbook, student and instructor toolkit, role-coding manual, data-protection/accessibility/evidence-governance guide, pilot protocol and readiness pack, five operational workbooks, prompt-security instruments, a JSON Schema, two worked examples, a semantic validator, and release verification.

Empirical sequence

Phase 1

Bounded feasibility and measurement study, approximately 120 students across two or three modules, with sample size determined by the primary estimand and precision target.

Phase 2

Multi-institution comparison across at least two and preferably three or more institutions and at least two academic cycles.

Scientific status

AIAP v6.5 is specification-complete for scholarly evaluation and governed pilot preparation. It is not empirically validated as an integrated institutional intervention. Publication, conformance documentation, schema validation, or workbook completion does not by itself authorize high-stakes use.
